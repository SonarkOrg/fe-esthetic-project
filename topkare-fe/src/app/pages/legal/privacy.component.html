How the information reaches us

When you use the contact form or the newsletter box, your details are handled in one of these ways:

Either way, your details reach us only as a message that we receive and read.

We keep enquiries for as long as needed to deal with them and for a reasonable period afterwards in case you get back in touch. We keep newsletter addresses until you ask us to remove them. You can ask us to delete your information sooner at any time.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your information. It is only seen by us and by the service providers that help us receive and store it, such as our email provider and, if configured, the form service mentioned above. Those providers handle it only to provide their service to us.
