The short version

{{ site.name }} does not set cookies of its own, and the site does not use analytics, advertising or tracking scripts. We do not store anything on your device to remember you between visits.

What this means in practice

  • No cookies are set by this website.
  • We do not use local storage, session storage or similar technologies to track you.
  • There is no login, so there is no session cookie.

Fonts loaded from Google

To display the site's typefaces and icons, your browser downloads font files from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com). This is a request to Google's servers, so Google receives your IP address and standard browser information, as with any website you visit. We do not control what Google does with that information; you can read how Google handles it in Google's own privacy policy.

Links to other sites

Our social media links (Instagram, X and Facebook) lead to third-party websites. Those sites may set their own cookies once you visit them, under their own policies.

Your browser settings

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, and you can use privacy tools that block third-party requests. The site should continue to work, although the typefaces may look different if the font files are blocked.
